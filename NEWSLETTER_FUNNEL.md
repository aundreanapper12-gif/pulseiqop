# PulseIQ Weekly Funnel

This sequence is designed for people who join through PulseIQ Insights or the Profit Leak Checklist.

## Email 1 — Immediately
**Subject:** Your PulseIQ Profit Leak Checklist

Thanks for joining PulseIQ Weekly. Start with the 15-point Profit Leak Checklist and circle the two or three areas that look most likely to deserve a closer look.

Do not try to cut every expense at once. Start with the largest recurring dollar impact, establish a baseline, and verify what changes next.

CTA: Open the Profit Leak Checklist
Secondary CTA: Take the Free Business Checkup

## Email 2 — Day 2
**Subject:** Revenue can grow while profit gets weaker

Higher sales do not automatically create stronger profit. Payroll, overtime, refunds, rework, acquisition cost, subscriptions, and vendor price increases can grow faster than revenue.

The useful question is not only “Did revenue grow?” It is “What changed underneath it?”

CTA: Read “Why Revenue Can Grow While Profit Shrinks”

## Email 3 — Day 4
**Subject:** Start with the biggest money signal

A long list of problems is not a plan. Rank findings by financial impact, confidence, and ease of investigation. Pick one or two priorities and assign a concrete next action.

CTA: Take the Free Business Checkup

## Email 4 — Day 7
**Subject:** What if you could test the change before making it?

PulseIQ lets owners compare actuals with targets, test scenarios, and track follow-up actions. The goal is to move from “I think this is costing us money” to a measurable hypothesis.

CTA: Start the 14-Day Trial

## Email 5 — Day 11
**Subject:** Your next 30 days: one focused profit improvement plan

Choose one baseline, one owner, one operating change, and one follow-up measure. The strongest improvement plans are narrow enough to measure.

CTA: Read the 30-Day Profit Improvement Plan
Secondary CTA: Start the 14-Day Trial

## Weekly Newsletter Format
1. One practical operating question.
2. One short explanation or calculation.
3. One example with transparent assumptions.
4. One action the reader can test this week.
5. One relevant PulseIQ article or tool CTA.

## Core Conversion Events
Track article view → checklist open → newsletter signup → checkup open → trial open → paid request/checkout.

## Email Platform Requirement
The site is already wired to POST subscriber email and source data to `NEWSLETTER_WEBHOOK_URL`. Configure that environment variable to point at the chosen email/CRM provider automation webhook. Do not log subscriber emails in application logs.
